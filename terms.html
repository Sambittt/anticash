Each individual user is permitted to create and operate exactly one account. Creating duplicate accounts, using family members' devices to repeat offers, or attempting to bypass restrictions constitutes fraud and results in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of all balances.

3. Prohibited activities

The following activities are strictly forbidden:

  • Using VPNs, proxies, Tor, or IP-altering software.
