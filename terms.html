1. Acceptance

By creating an account, signing in with Google or using AntiCash, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

2. One account per person

Each person may have one account. Duplicate accounts, using other people's devices to repeat offers, or trying to get around limits is fraud. It leads to suspension and loss of all balances.

3. Prohibited activity

  • Using VPNs, proxies, Tor or other tools that hide your location.
  • Using emulators such as BlueStacks or Nox, virtual machines or scripts.
  • Giving false or contradictory survey answers.
  • Tampering with partner callbacks or postback requests.

Accounts that break these rules are banned permanently, and pending and available funds are forfeited.

4. Confirmations and reversals

  • Partner approval. Earnings depend on completions verified by partners. Confirmation can take up to 7 days.
  • Reversals. If a partner checks an offer and finds the instructions were not met, or that a proxy was used, the partner cancels the reward and the amount is deducted from your wallet.

5. Withdrawals

  • The minimum withdrawal is ₹300.00 (30,000 paise).
  • Withdrawals are reviewed and paid by UPI within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • You are responsible for your UPI ID being correct and active. We cannot recover money sent to an address you entered wrongly.
  • Only one pending withdrawal request is allowed at a time.

6. Short links

  • What you may link to. Only content you are allowed to share. Malware, phishing, scams, adult content, pirated material, hate or violence, and anything illegal in India are not allowed.
  • What counts. A view counts once per visitor per day, only after the visitor waits for the timer and only from real browsers. Your own views, repeat views, bots, traffic you buy or incentivise, and views from links to blocked sites are not counted.
  • Rates. Rates are paid per 1,000 counted views and depend on the visitor country. They are starting rates and can change; the rate shown when you read this is not a promise for future views.
  • Our rights. We can disable any link, remove its earnings and ban the account, with or without notice, when these rules are broken or ad partners ask us to.
  • Earnings. Link earnings are Pending for up to 7 days, like offer earnings, and can be reversed if traffic is found to be invalid.

7. Taxes

You are responsible for reporting your earnings and paying any tax due under Indian law. Ask a tax professional how the rules apply to you.
