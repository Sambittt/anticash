About

A rewards service with plain rules.

We connect people in India with paid partner offers, and we pay out in rupees to UPI.

Our modelRealistic expectationsWhy we do not use Google AdSenseOur standards

Our model

Advertisers, game publishers and market research firms look for feedback and app testing from Indian audiences. They distribute those offers through affiliate networks known as offerwalls.

When you complete a survey or an app offer through one of our partners, that partner pays AntiCash a placement fee. We share that revenue with you in rupees, paid to your UPI ID once the offer is confirmed.

Realistic expectations

AntiCash is for small, occasional earnings in your spare time. It is not a job, a salary or a dependable main income. The offers you can see change from day to day with advertiser budgets and targeting.

Why we do not use Google AdSense

Google AdSense policies prohibit rewarding people for viewing or clicking ads. AntiCash follows that rule and shows no AdSense in the rewards flow. Everything you earn comes from partner offerwalls and research panels that allow rewarded participation.

Our standards

  • Real rupees. No virtual tokens or points. You see and earn rupees.
  • Direct UPI payouts. Money goes to the UPI ID you enter.
  • Honest numbers. Sample screens on this site are labeled as samples. We do not publish made-up earnings or reviews.
  • No abuse. To keep partners paying, we remove proxies, VPN use, emulators and duplicate accounts.
