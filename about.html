About AntiCash

Last Updated: November 2026 • Domain: anticash.tech

The Thesis

For thousands of years, physical cash allowed people to trade freely, privately, and without permission from a central entity. When commerce migrated to the web, we gained unprecedented global reach, but at an immense cost: our transactions became tracked, monetized, restricted, and taxed by layers of financial middlemen.

AntiCash was founded on a simple observation: we don't need to return to paper banknotes, nor do we have to accept invasive commercial surveillance. We can build a modern financial substrate that embodies the sovereign characteristics of cash—frictionless, private, and instantaneous—amplified by the speed of modern technology.

Our Core Principles

"If physical cash were invented today, traditional gatekeepers would try to outlaw it. We are building its digital successor."

  • Zero Friction: Transfers should finalize in milliseconds, anywhere in the world, with negligible overhead.
  • Built-In Privacy: Financial privacy is a fundamental human right. Transaction data should never be harvested or sold to data brokers.
  • Open Standards: AntiCash is engineered with transparency and open interoperability as first-class citizens.
  • Direct Custody: You retain ownership of your assets without exposure to counterparty insolvency or arbitrary account freezes.
