About AntiCash

A transparent rewards service connecting users in India with advertiser research.

Our model

Digital advertisers, game publishers, and market research firms regularly seek consumer feedback and application testing from Indian audiences. These companies distribute offers through specialized affiliate networks known as offerwalls.

When you complete a survey or install a trial app through an offerwall partner, that partner pays AntiCash a placement fee. We share this revenue directly with you in Indian rupees, payable to your UPI virtual payment address once verified.

Realistic expectations

AntiCash is designed for small discretionary earnings in your spare time. It is not an employment contract, salary, or dependable primary income source. Available surveys and app tests fluctuate daily depending on advertiser budgets and demographic targets.

Why Google AdSense is not used

Google AdSense policies strictly prohibit incentivizing users to view, click, or interact with advertisements. AntiCash complies with these standards and does not display Google AdSense in any rewards flow. All compensation comes exclusively from performance-marketing offerwalls and research panels that explicitly allow rewarded user engagement.

Our standards

  • Direct currency: We do not hide earnings behind artificial virtual tokens. You see and earn money in Indian rupees.
  • Direct UPI payouts: Funds are transferred directly to your bank account via UPI.
  • Zero tolerance for abuse: To maintain relationships with advertisers, we actively detect and ban proxies, VPNs, emulators, and duplicate accounts.
