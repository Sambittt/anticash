About AntiCash

A transparent, no-nonsense rewards platform engineered for users in India.

Our Mission: Absolute Transparency

The web is filled with reward websites promising extravagant earnings, fake live payout tickers, and unrealistic guarantees. AntiCash is built on a totally different principle: absolute honesty.

AntiCash connects consumers in India with global advertising networks, app developers, and market research agencies (known in the digital industry as offerwalls). When companies launch a new consumer app, game, or questionnaire in India, they need real humans to test features, provide demographic feedback, and try products.

How the Economics Work

When you complete a survey or install a trial application:

  1. The advertiser pays AntiCash a fee for your verified engagement.
  2. We share the majority of that advertising revenue directly with you in the form of redeemable AntiCash points.
  3. You convert those points directly into Indian Rupees sent via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or digital retail vouchers.
💡
Realistic Expectations: AntiCash is designed to help you earn modest pocket-money rewards during your commute or downtime. It is not a salary, job, or get-rich-quick opportunity. Available offers vary daily depending on demographic demand from advertisers.

Why We Do Not Use Google AdSense for Rewards

Many low-quality sites try to place Google AdSense ads on reward pages or ask users to click banners to earn coins. AntiCash strictly forbids this practice.

Google AdSense policies strictly prohibit incentivizing users to view, click, or engage with ads. AntiCash works exclusively with compliant performance-marketing offerwall networks and survey panels that are explicitly intended for rewarded engagement.

Our Core Commitments to You

  • No Fake Payout Proofs: We never display fake popups showing "User X just withdrew ₹5,000". What you see on AntiCash is 100% genuine.
  • Clear Status Tracking: Every offer you complete has a transparent status (Pending, Confirmed, or Reversed) with clear dates and transaction IDs.
  • Direct UPI Payments: You don't have to jump through international payment processors. Payouts are made directly in Indian Rupees to your UPI ID.
  • Zero Tolerance for Fraud: To protect our relationships with advertising partners, we actively monitor and ban automated bots, VPNs, and duplicate accounts.
