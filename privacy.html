Privacy policy.

Effective date: November 24, 2026. Website: anticash.tech

1. Information we collect2. Partner offerwalls3. Advertising policy3a. Short-link visitors4. Data security5. Access and deletion

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to sign you in and pay you:

  • Sign-in data. When you sign in with Google, Firebase gives us your email, display name and a unique user ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Payment details. When you request a withdrawal, we store the UPI ID you enter, only to send the payment.
  • Wallet history. Your completed offers, pending and available balances and withdrawal requests are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Technical logs. When a partner sends us a completion notice, we log the source IP address and time to check it is genuine and to prevent proxy abuse.

2. Partner offerwalls

We work with independent research and app testing partners. When you open an offer from the Earn page:

  • We pass your anonymous member ID so the partner can tell us when you finish.
  • We do not share your email address or UPI ID with partners.
  • Partners have their own privacy notices. Read them before you answer surveys or install software.

3. Advertising policy

AntiCash does not use Google AdSense in the rewards flow or on short-link pages. You are never paid to click or view Google ads. Rewards come from verified completions on partner offerwalls, and short-link earnings come from ads shown by advertising partners on the link page.

Information pages such as About, Privacy, Terms and Contact may show Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads; you can manage this at adssettings.google.com.

3a. Short-link visitors

When you open a short link, our server sees your IP address and browser details. We use them to count views fairly and to stop abuse. We store a one-way hash of your IP address, not the address itself, together with your approximate country. The advertising partners on the link page may set their own cookies.

4. Data security

Records are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. Changes to balances and transactions can only be made by our server-side functions, and database security rules block the browser from making them.

5. Access and deletion

To see your data or ask us to delete your account, email support@anticash.tech from the Google address on your account.
