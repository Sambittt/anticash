Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Website: https://anticash.tech

1. Overview

At AntiCash ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible from anticash.tech, your privacy is fundamental. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use our rewards platform, how it is processed, and your rights under applicable Indian data privacy regulations and international standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information strictly required to operate your account and process UPI rewards:

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in using Google Auth, Firebase provides us with your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID). We do not receive or store your Google account password.
  • Payment Information (UPI ID / Email): When you submit a withdrawal request, we collect the UPI ID (e.g. user@bank) or gift card delivery email address solely to disburse your funds.
  • IP Address & Device Telemetry: When third-party offerwall networks send completion postbacks, we log the user's source IP address and completion timestamp to prevent fraud and multi-account abuse.
  • Points & Transaction Records: We maintain a ledger of your task completions, pending points, confirmed points, and withdrawal history in Google Cloud Firestore.

3. Third-Party Offerwall Partners

AntiCash connects with independent digital advertising partners and market research panels (offerwalls). When you browse or launch a task from our Tasks page:

  • We transmit your pseudonymous AntiCash UID as a tracking sub-identifier (e.g. ?uid=YOUR_UID) so the partner network can notify our servers when you finish the task.
  • We never share your email address, legal name, or UPI ID with offerwall networks.
  • Partner networks operate under their own independent privacy policies. We encourage you to review their terms before participating in surveys or downloading third-party applications.

4. Prohibition of Google AdSense in Rewards Flow

🛡️
Advertising Policy Disclosure: AntiCash strictly adheres to advertising network guidelines. We do NOT use Google AdSense anywhere in the rewards flow, and we never reward or pay users for viewing or clicking Google ads. All rewards are derived exclusively from verified task completions on compliant, third-party offerwall platforms.

5. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

We use session cookies and local storage exclusively for essential user authentication (Firebase Auth tokens) and remembering your navigation preferences. We do not sell user behavioral data to data brokers.

6. Security & Data Retention

Your account data and transaction history are stored securely on Google Cloud Platform (Firebase Auth and Firestore). All database write permissions for balances and transactions are strictly locked down by server-side Firestore Security Rules and can only be modified through secure Cloud Functions.

7. Account Deletion & Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data or ask for the deletion of your AntiCash account and associated records. To submit a request, contact our support team at support@anticash.tech using the Google email associated with your account.
