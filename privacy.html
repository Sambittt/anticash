Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Website: https://anticash.tech

1. Information we collect

We collect minimal information necessary to authenticate accounts and disburse UPI payments:

  • Authentication data: When you sign in with Google, Firebase provides your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID). We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Payment details: When requesting a payout, we record your provided UPI virtual payment address solely to send funds.
  • Transaction history: Records of your completed tasks, pending balance, confirmed balance, and withdrawal requests are retained in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Technical logs: When an offerwall sends a postback, we log the user source IP address and timestamp to verify completion legitimacy and prevent proxy abuse.

2. Third-party offerwall networks

AntiCash integrates with independent market research and application testing panels. When you open a task from our Earn page:

  • We forward your pseudonymous user UID so the partner can notify our backend when you finish the task.
  • We do not share your email address or UPI address with offerwall networks.
  • Offerwall networks operate under their own independent privacy notices. Review their specific disclosures before submitting survey responses or testing third-party software.

3. Advertising policy disclosure

AntiCash does not use Google AdSense anywhere in the rewards flow. Users are never paid or rewarded for clicking or viewing Google advertisements. All rewards are derived exclusively from verified completions on compliant affiliate offerwall platforms.

4. Data security

Account records are stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. All database writes for user balances and transactions are locked down by server-side Firestore security rules and can only be executed by Cloud Functions.

5. Account deletion requests

You may request access to your data or request deletion of your account by emailing support@anticash.tech from the Google email address associated with your profile.
