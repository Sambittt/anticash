Tips for getting credited

Advertisers verify completions automatically through tracking identifiers. Follow these practices to avoid cancelled rewards:

  • Finish every requirement: Read all offer criteria before clicking. Some tasks require completing a tutorial, registering an account, or verifying your email.
  • Do not use VPNs, proxies, or Tor: Partner tracking systems reject requests routed through data centers or proxy networks. Accounts using VPNs are banned.
  • Keep apps installed: For mobile application testing, keep the installed application open for the required duration and do not uninstall immediately after download.
  • Use the same personal device: Do not start an offer on your mobile phone and finish it on a laptop. Complete the entire flow within the same browser session.
