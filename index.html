Rules & payouts

Everything in one quick table.

Minimum withdrawal
₹300.00 in Available balance. No platform fee is deducted.
Payout method

Pending period

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is it free?
Yes. Signing up, offers and short links are free. We never ask you to pay to join or to withdraw.
How much can I earn?
It varies with the offers available to you and the views your links get. We do not promise a figure, and we show no made-up earnings.
When is my money Available?
New earnings are Pending until the partner confirms them, which can take up to 7 days. Then they move to Available, which counts toward your ₹300 minimum.
How do withdrawals work?
From ₹300 Available, enter your UPI ID on the Withdraw page. We review every request and pay within 24 to 48 business hours.
How do short links pay?
You paste a web address and get a short one. Visitors see a short page with ads, then continue to your link. Valid views earn money by visitor country. Repeat views, bots and blocked destinations do not count.
What links are not allowed?
Malware, phishing, scams, adult content, pirated material and anything illegal in India. We disable such links, remove their earnings and can ban the account. You can report a link from its page or from the contact page.
Why did an offer reverse?
Partners re-check offers. If instructions were not followed, or a VPN or emulator was detected, the partner cancels the reward and we deduct it. See the terms.
Is my data safe?
We keep only what we need: your Google sign-in details, wallet history and the UPI ID you enter. Only our server-side code can change balances. See the privacy policy.
