Got Questions?

Transparent Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in complete openness about earnings, verification timelines, and rules.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum threshold is 500 points (equal to ₹50). Once your confirmed balance reaches 500 points, you can submit a withdrawal request directly to any valid Indian UPI ID (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.) or select a gift card voucher.

What is the difference between Pending and Confirmed points?

When you finish an offer, the partner network notifies us and points enter your account as Pending. Advertiser partners require a verification hold period (typically up to 7 days) to ensure the survey was answered legitimately or that the app was tested according to their requirements. Once verified, points automatically become Confirmed and eligible for withdrawal.

Why can partner confirmations take days?

Advertisers process completions in batch cycles to screen out automated bots, fake survey inputs, and quick uninstalls. They only release funds once quality checks are satisfied. We pass the rewards to you the moment partners confirm the completion.

Can points ever be reversed or deducted?

Yes. If an advertising partner detects that an offer was not completed according to their terms (for example, providing inconsistent survey answers, using a VPN, or submitting fraudulent information), the partner cancels the payout. When a partner issues a chargeback/reversal, corresponding points are subtracted.

Can I use a VPN, multiple accounts, or bots?

Strictly NO. Partner networks utilize advanced anti-fraud filters. If VPNs, proxies, Tor, emulators, or multiple accounts are detected, the system immediately flags and bans the account. All pending and confirmed points will be permanently forfeited.

Are rewards taxable in India?

Yes. You are solely responsible for declaring your earnings and paying any applicable taxes according to Indian Income Tax regulations.
