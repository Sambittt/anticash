Ways to earn

Partner networks offer varied tasks. Choose campaigns matching your device and preferences.

Surveys

Answer consumer questionnaires from market research agencies about products, shopping habits, and media. Questions take between 5 and 20 minutes to complete.

App offers

Install, register, or test new mobile applications according to specified advertiser criteria. Keep apps open for required test periods to ensure tracking registers.

Game offers

How it works

Four simple steps from signing up to receiving your UPI payment.

01

Sign in with Google

Create your profile in one click with Google authentication. We enforce a strict one account per person policy to preserve network integrity. No passwords required.

02

Pick an offer and complete every requirement

Browse partner offerwalls, select a survey or task, and read all requirements carefully. Complete the task honestly on your personal device without using VPNs or proxies.

03

Partner confirms it (Pending hold)

When you finish, the partner records the completion and credits your pending balance. Advertisers audit tasks over a standard review window (up to 7 days) before moving funds to your confirmed balance.

04

Request UPI withdrawal once Available reaches Rs 300

Enter your Indian UPI ID on the Withdraw page. Payout requests are verified and disbursed directly to your bank account within 24 to 48 business hours.

Payout details

Transparent breakdown of our payment parameters, processing times, and rules.

Minimum withdrawal Rs 300.00 (30,000 paise). Once your confirmed balance reaches this threshold, you can request a transfer.
Payout method Direct UPI transfer to your Virtual Payment Address (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or bank UPI).
Processing time Withdrawal requests are manually reviewed for fraudulent activity and disbursed within 24 to 48 business hours.
Pending period Advertisers hold new completions in pending status for up to 7 days to run quality control and prevent automated fraud.
Reversals If an advertiser determines an offer was finished fraudulently, using proxies, or without completing requirements, pending funds are deducted.
Accounts Strictly one account per individual. Using VPNs, proxies, emulators, or multiple profiles results in immediate permanent suspension.
Disclaimer: Earnings depend on available partner offers. AntiCash is not a job or guaranteed daily income. Complete tasks in your spare time.

What we will never do

AntiCash is designed with complete security and transparency. Protect yourself from online impostors by knowing our commitments:

  • Never ask for your UPI PIN, passwords, or OTP: We only need your public UPI VPA address to send money to you.
  • Never ask you to pay money or deposit funds: AntiCash is 100% free. We never charge registration fees or premium membership upgrades.
  • Never require side-loading unverified APK files: All tested applications must be downloaded from official app stores like Google Play.
  • Never promise fixed daily income: Rewards fluctuate based on available partner research. We never promise guaranteed salaries.
Encountered suspicious activity? Report it directly to support@anticash.tech.
