Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pending and confirmed balance?

When you complete an offer, the partner reports the transaction and the amount appears in your pending balance. Advertisers hold completions for a quality check period (usually up to 7 days) to ensure answers were valid. Once the hold period ends without dispute, the funds move to your confirmed balance and can be withdrawn.

How do I receive payments?

Navigate to the Withdraw page, enter your verified Indian UPI ID (e.g. name@bank), and submit. Payouts are reviewed and sent directly to your bank account via UPI within 24 to 48 business hours.

Why was my offer completion delayed or reversed?

Advertisers audit submissions against strict quality controls. If a user speeds through questions, provides conflicting demographic data, or fails to fulfill all app testing steps, the partner will reject the transaction.

Are taxes deducted from withdrawals?

You are personally responsible for reporting your earnings and paying any applicable income tax in accordance with Indian tax laws.
